01 — Script rehearsal

Run lines with a scene partner who never gets tired.

Upload your play as a Word file and pick your character. Playmate reads everyone else's lines aloud, then waits while you say yours and carries on when you go quiet.

Script layouts Playmate understands

Name and colon before each speech:

MARGARET: You're late again.
TOM: (out of breath) The bus broke down.

Or the name on its own line, speech underneath:

MARGARET
You're late again.

A two-column table (name | speech) also works. Lines in (parentheses), [brackets] or italics count as stage directions and are shown but not read.
